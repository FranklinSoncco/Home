Mechatronics Engineer — Robotics, Industrial Automation & Artificial Intelligence

Universidad de Ingeniería y Tecnología (UTEC) · Lima, Peru

I am a Mechatronics Engineer trained at UTEC, with an institutional specialisation in Advanced Robotics, Industrial Mechatronics and Artificial Intelligence. My work sits at the intersection of modern control theory, machine learning and autonomous robotics, applied to agriculture, mining, rehabilitation and space exploration.

I have authored two peer-reviewed review articles indexed in SCOPUS, led a research team on cervical exoskeletons, and delivered instrumentation and control projects on Siemens, Rockwell, FESTO and Quanser platforms. Most recently I completed an engineering internship at Minera Alpayana, working on technology scouting and instrumentation.

Areas of Specialisation

Institutional specialisation, UTEC

Robotics

Humanoid, quadruped, aerial and mobile robots with custom end-effectors, and the modern and nonlinear control that drives them — optimisation-based control, LQR/LQG, MPC, sliding mode and impedance control — together with SLAM for autonomous navigation. Applied to agriculture, space exploration, rehabilitation and mining.

Industrial Automation

PLC programming and SCADA supervision, P&ID design to ISA standards, sizing and selection of sensors and actuators, and industrial networks — Siemens, Rockwell, FESTO, Endress+Hauser and Schneider Electric. Mining is the field that interests me most.

Artificial Intelligence

Supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning; deep learning from convolutional networks to transformers and generative models; and computer vision — applied to robot perception, time-series classification and predictive maintenance.
