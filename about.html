About

Professional Profile

My motivation is to work as a researcher in advanced robotics, integrating the most recent technology with the fundamentals of robotics itself. I hold the institutional specialisation in Advanced Robotics, Industrial Mechatronics and Artificial Intelligence from UTEC, and my work concentrates on three areas.

Robotics
Humanoid, quadruped and mobile robots with custom end-effectors, and the modern control that drives them — optimisation-based, neural-network-based and dynamic-model-based — together with SLAM for autonomous navigation. My application fields are agriculture, space exploration, rehabilitation medicine and mining.
Industrial Automation
Backed by my specialisation in Industrial Mechatronics: reading and implementing industrial process drawings to standard, and sizing the sensors and actuators a given process requires. Mining is the field I am most drawn to, alongside any environment where a process can be automated.
Artificial Intelligence
Machine learning, deep learning and reinforcement learning — the areas of technology that most excite me today — applied to perception, predictive maintenance and control. I want to create knowledge and build technology that makes everyday human tasks easier.
Research & Teaching
Research is what I am most passionate about, and teaching is where I found I can communicate it. Advising students across three national programmes, their feedback consistently reported understanding the material better than in their regular classes.
